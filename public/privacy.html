1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how [COMPANY NAME] (“Lighthouse,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you interact with the Lighthouse service available at getcompset.app (the “Service”), including the Lighthouse opt-in form and SMS alerts. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms of Service.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Information you provide

  • Mobile phone number when you submit the opt-in form and complete SMS verification.
  • SMS replies you send to the Lighthouse phone number, including feedback, STOP requests, and HELP requests.
  • Account profile information (such as your name, role, organization, time zone, and alert hours) provided by your property administrator or entered through the Service.

Information collected automatically

  • Delivery and engagement metadata for SMS messages sent to or received from you (for example, message identifier, timestamp, delivery status).
  • Service logs generated by our infrastructure (for example, error logs and IP addresses associated with administrative access to the Service).

Information from third parties

  • Competitor rate data collected from publicly available booking platforms. This data does not identify individual consumers; it relates to room rates, availability, and room categories for hospitality properties monitored by the Service.

3. How we use information

  • To operate and provide the Service, including sending pricing alerts you opt in to receive;
  • To verify your phone number and confirm your consent to receive SMS messages;
  • To respond to inbound SMS feedback and to your support requests;
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or technical issues;
  • To comply with legal obligations and to enforce our Terms of Service.

4. SMS opt-in and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subprocessors that support our SMS delivery (for example, our messaging provider) is permitted only for the purpose of delivering and operating the Service. All other categories exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We use your phone number and opt-in record solely to deliver the SMS alerts you signed up for and to maintain a record of your consent. Opt-in records include the phone number, the consent checkbox state, the timestamp of consent, and the version of the opt-in form you completed.

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. After we process your opt-out, your phone number remains in our suppression records to honor your request, but you will not receive further alerts unless you opt back in.

5. How we share information

We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers (subprocessors). We share necessary information with vendors who help us operate the Service, including:
    • Twilio (SMS delivery and phone number verification);
    • Our cloud hosting and database providers;
    • Error monitoring and logging providers.
    These vendors are contractually limited to using information only to perform services for us.
  • Your organization. Your account information, SMS opt-in status, and inbound feedback may be visible to your organization’s administrators within the Service.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Lighthouse, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred subject to the protections of this policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for the following additional periods where required for legal, compliance, or operational reasons:

  • SMS opt-in records: retained while your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward to demonstrate consent.
  • Delivery and engagement metadata: retained as required by our messaging provider and our compliance obligations.
  • Service logs and rate snapshots: retained for operational and audit purposes.

When retention is no longer necessary, we delete or de-identify the information.
